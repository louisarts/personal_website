LLM Noise Injection Pipeline

Applied NLP Internship · LemonAI, London · Jan – May 2025

Python · Claude API · NLP · Data Engineering

LemonAI was a small London-based AI startup working on transcription and speech NLP. I joined as an applied NLP intern from January to May 2025. The core problem I worked on was that NLP models trained on clean transcripts often degrade in production because real speech output is full of noise that doesn't appear in training data: filled pauses, repetitions, phonetic substitutions, and disfluencies. To close that gap, I designed and built a pipeline to inject realistic linguistic noise into clean transcription data, expanding training distributions without requiring additional labelled examples.

The pipeline combined a rule-based module for deterministic, high-volume noise injection with an LLM component using the Claude API for generative noise that produces naturalistic, contextually coherent variation, the kind of disfluency that rules alone can't replicate. Working at a small startup meant taking ownership end-to-end: scoping the problem, surveying prior work, designing the system architecture, and iterating quickly without much process overhead. It taught me how research translates into production-facing tooling, and gave me a much clearer sense of how LLMs can be used as components inside larger data pipelines rather than as standalone tools.

About

I'm an MSc student in Data Science and Machine Learning at UCL, focused on NLP and computer vision. Currently a research intern at Chattermill and co-author of research on cross-lingual factual recall in LLMs, under review at EMNLP 2026. Before ML I trained as a physicist — an MSc in Astrophysics at Imperial College London — and built NLP data pipelines at Lemon AI, a London startup. Driven to build genuinely useful models.

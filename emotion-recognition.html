Real-Time Facial Emotion Recognition

Team project · Presented at Le Wagon Demo Day, Zurich 2022

GitHub ↗

This was my first ever machine learning project, and the one that got me genuinely hooked on AI. Built during Le Wagon — an intensive data science bootcamp I did in Zurich in late 2022 — it was a team project over the final two weeks of the programme: AIdentify, a real-time facial emotion recognition system built from scratch in under two weeks with a team of three.

The system detects faces in live webcam video and classifies the expressed emotion across eight categories: neutral, happy, sad, surprised, fearful, angry, disgust, and contempt.

Model and Data

We trained on a combined, deduplicated dataset of approximately 40,000 labelled images (FER+ and AffectNet-HQ), applying data augmentation and class weighting to handle label imbalance across categories. A custom CNN baseline reached 71% validation accuracy. Fine-tuning a pretrained DenseNet169 via transfer learning — with frozen convolutional weights and a retrained classifier head — pushed that to 81%.

System

The inference pipeline runs OpenCV face detection into a FastAPI prediction endpoint, streamed live through a Flask web application. We presented at Le Wagon Demo Day in Zurich in December 2022, and it's still the project I point to as the moment I realised I wanted to keep building things like this.
